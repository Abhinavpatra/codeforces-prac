### Core progression

**189A → 455A → 698A → 607A → 1133E → 466C → 1114D → 607B → 118D → 319C**

If you can solve those 10 comfortably, you'll have covered most of the fundamental DP patterns.

Rule: try each problem for 30–45 minutes before looking at hints. After solving it, identify the state, transition, base case, and time complexity. That habit will help much more than simply accumulating accepted submissions.
